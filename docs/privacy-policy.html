Lugha Translator privacy policy

Effective September 26, 2026

Lugha Translator ("Lugha," "the app") is a translation app for African languages and major international languages such as English, French, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, and Mandarin, developed by PDTech Labs. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and who it is shared with.

In short: Lugha has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Text and photos you choose to translate are sent to an AI service to produce the translation, and your translation history stays on your device.

Information the app handles

Text you enter. When you translate, detect a language, or request a pronunciation guide, the text you typed or pasted is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the result.

Photos you choose. When you use the camera button, the photo you take or select is sent to Anthropic's Claude API so the text in it can be read and translated. Photos are only accessed when you tap the camera button and pick or take one. The app does not save your photos.

Translation history. Your past translations (the original text, the translation, and the languages used) are saved locally on your device so you can view them in the History tab. This history is not uploaded to any server run by the developer.

Text-to-speech. When you play a translation aloud, the app uses your device's built-in speech features.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account, name, email address, or phone number
  • No location data
  • No contacts, messages, or files beyond a photo you choose to translate
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics, or tracking

How your information is shared

Text and photos you submit for translation are sent to Anthropic, the provider of the Claude AI model that performs the translation. Anthropic processes this data to return the result and handles it under its own policies. You can read Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

The developer does not sell your information and does not share it with anyone else.

All data sent from the app is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Permissions

Camera — used only to take a photo of text you want translated.

Photos — used only to let you select an image of text you want translated.

You can deny or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. Typed translation works without them.

Keeping and deleting your data

The developer does not store your text, photos, or history on any server. Your translation history lives only on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Children

Lugha is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
